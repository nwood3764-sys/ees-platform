Measure quantity overrides
Populated from the reports (attic = Total Gross Roof Area; water savers = dwelling units) so you can see exactly what will print. Edit any value — e.g. to match the offer letter — and the entered number takes precedence on every document.
HEAR — Appliance & Equipment Rebates (mechanical) — the HEAR scope, separate from the HOMES proposal. Add each system/appliance with its model number, efficiency and quantity.
Low-income (≤80% AMI): the program pays 100% of the project cost, up to the ENERGY STAR product-category caps (Heat Pump for Space Heating/Cooling $8,000 · Heat Pump Water Heater $1,750 · Heat Pump Clothes Dryer $840 · Electric Cooking Appliance $840 · Insulation, Air Sealing, and Ventilation $1,600 · Electrical Panel $4,000 · Electric Wiring $2,500) and $14,000 maximum per dwelling unit. Same in North Carolina and Wisconsin.
